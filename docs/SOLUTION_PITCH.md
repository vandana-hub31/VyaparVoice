# VyaparVoice – Your Shop, Ready to Speak

## Problem
Small shop owners often manage sales, inventory, customer credit, and expenses manually. Existing digital tools can be difficult to use, especially for shopkeepers who prefer speaking in their regional languages.

## Our Solution
VyaparVoice is an AI-powered, multilingual voice-based business management assistant designed for small Indian shopkeepers. It allows users to manage daily shop activities through simple voice commands and a user-friendly dashboard.

## Key Features
- **Multilingual Voice Assistant:** Supports English, Kannada, Hindi, and Malayalam voice interactions.
- **Sales Management:** Record sales and track daily revenue.
- **POS Billing:** Generate bills and invoices.
- **Inventory Management:** Monitor stock and update product quantities.
- **Customer Ledger:** Track customer balances and credit.
- **Expense Tracking:** Record and categorize business expenses.
- **Business Analytics:** View sales, profit estimates, and daily summaries.

## Technology Stack
- Frontend: Next.js, React, TypeScript
- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- Database: PostgreSQL
- AI Integration: Gnani AI speech technology and intent processing

## How It Works
1. The shopkeeper gives a voice command.
2. Speech-to-text technology converts speech into text.
3. The backend identifies the user's intent.
4. The application performs the required operation or retrieves information from PostgreSQL.
5. The result is displayed and communicated to the user.

## Innovation and Impact
VyaparVoice makes digital business management more accessible through voice interaction. It aims to reduce manual recordkeeping, save time, improve stock tracking, and help small shopkeepers understand their business activities more easily.
